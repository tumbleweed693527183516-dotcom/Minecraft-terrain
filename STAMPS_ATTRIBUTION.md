# Elevation data attribution

Vivid Lands' real-world mountain stamps are derived from the open **Terrain Tiles** dataset
(https://registry.opendata.aws/terrain-tiles/), which combines:

- SRTM - NASA / USGS (public domain)
- 3DEP / NED - U.S. Geological Survey (public domain)
- ETOPO1 - NOAA
- EU-DEM - produced using Copernicus data and information funded by the European Union
- ArcticDEM - Polar Geospatial Center (University of Minnesota), NSF / NGA
- Other national datasets listed at https://github.com/tilezen/joerd/blob/master/docs/attribution.md

The data has been cropped, resampled, rescaled and feathered for use as game terrain.
